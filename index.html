Three wellness ideas

Take a walk

Take a short walk to move your body and take a break from screens.

Journal

Journaling can help you sort out your thoughts and set goals.

Meditate

Try a meditation that fits your mood and helps you feel more present.

Featured: Meditation

Guided meditations come in many lengths and can match different moods and feelings. You can find free ones on YouTube, whether you have a few minutes or a longer break.

Quick reset between classes

Drink some water, stretch your shoulders, and take a few slow breaths before your next class.

Wind down before bed

Put away your phone, dim the lights, and give yourself a few quiet minutes to relax.
